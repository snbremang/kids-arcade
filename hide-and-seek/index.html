How to Play

  • WASD — Move
  • Left Click — Shoot (when you're the seeker)
As a Hider
  • Hide behind structures to avoid the seeker
  • Collect fruit/stars during fruit call phases (+5 pts each)
  • Survive the seeking phase to earn points (1 pt/sec)
  • You have 3 lives — lose them all and you become the seeker
As the Seeker
  • Stay inside the golden circle
  • Shoot hiders to earn 5 points per hit
  • Call a fruit or wildcard during fruit call phases

Game lasts 4 minutes. Highest score wins!
